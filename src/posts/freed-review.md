Freed is an AI medical scribe for individual clinicians and small practices. It listens to a consultation, drafts the note in a template for your specialty, and lets you edit before you copy it into the record.

**Status: researched, not yet tested.** This review is based on Freed's own pricing page, checked on 17 October 2026. Everything in it is Freed's claim, not our finding, until we test it. If you want the wider picture first, see our [shortlist of AI scribes for US doctors](/ai-scribes/best-ai-scribes-us/).

## What Freed does

According to its pricing page, Freed gives you:

- an AI scribe with specialty-specific templates
- a template builder (Core and above)
- Clinical Evidence answers (Core and above)
- visit summaries, patient instructions, letters and referrals (Premier and above)
- dictation and saved phrases (Premier and above)
- ICD-10 and CPT coding suggestions (Premier and above)
- one-click EHR push into browser-based EHRs (Premier and above)

Freed says it can capture patient speech in languages including Spanish, Mandarin, Italian, German, Russian, Japanese, French, Korean, Thai, Portuguese, Dutch, Swedish, Polish and Indonesian, and write the note in English.

## Who it suits

The plans are set up for a clinician working alone or in a small group. A resident, student or trainee can apply for Core for free through a separate form, and students can get a 50% discount on the monthly individual plan after verification, on direct purchases only. Large health systems will probably look at the enterprise tools in our [AI scribes hub](/ai-scribes/) instead.

## Pricing

| Plan | Published price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | From US$39/month | Up to 40 notes a month; templates; live support |
| Core | US$59–79/month | Unlimited notes; template builder; Clinical Evidence answers |
| Premier | US$104–119/month | Adds summaries, patient letters, EHR push, dictation, coding |
| Groups | On request | Adds assistant users, note sharing, single sign-on, admin dashboards, account manager |

The page shows two figures for Core and for Premier, and does not label which belongs to monthly or annual billing. The ranges above reflect that, so check the price at checkout. Freed also sells a separate Front Desk product from US$149 a month. All prices were checked on 17 October 2026. Confirm on the vendor's site before you buy.

## Privacy and compliance

Freed describes itself as HIPAA-compliant, HITECH-compliant and SOC 2 Type II certified. These are Freed's statements; we have not seen the audit reports.

- **United States:** an organisation-wide business associate agreement is listed under Groups. If you are on a single-user plan, ask Freed in writing how a BAA is handled.
- **Audio:** the page says audio is saved temporarily until summaries and quality checks are complete and then deleted. It also says Freed does not store patient recordings. Ask Freed to state its retention rule in one sentence.
- **United Kingdom:** Freed is not among the 19 suppliers NHS England named when it launched its AI scribe registry in January 2026. NHS practices should ask Freed about its status. See [our NHS guide](/ai-scribes/nhs-ambient-voice-technology-guidance/).
- **Canada and Australia:** the pricing page does not say which countries are supported or where data is stored. Ask about data residency and local privacy law before you start.

Always tell patients the consultation is being recorded and get their agreement, as your local rules require.

## Limits to check

- **Note cap:** 40 notes a month will not cover a full-time clinic, so most full-time clinicians will need Core or above.
- **EHR fit:** push into the record is a Premier feature. Check that it works with your EHR before you pay.
- **Accuracy:** we have no test results yet. During the trial, run it on your own real consultations and count the edits each note needs.
- **You stay responsible:** the AI drafts, and you review and sign every note.

## Alternatives

- **Heidi:** has a free plan, so you can try ambient scribing at no cost. See the [shortlist](/ai-scribes/best-ai-scribes-us/).
- **Enterprise tools** such as Abridge, Suki and Microsoft Dragon Copilot suit health systems and have no published prices.
